Comprehensive Rule 6 Coverage

Evaluated strictly according to the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011.

Rule 6(1)(a)

Manufacturer & Packer

Complete corporate name, street address, state, and postal PIN code verification.

Rule 6(1)(da)

MRP (Incl. of All Taxes)

Verification of numeric retail sale price in INR and mandatory tax inclusive phrase.

Rule 6(1)(c) & 12

Net Quantity (Metric SI)

Strict verification of authorized standard SI units (g, kg, ml, l). Rejection of non-metric units.

Rule 6(1)(d)

Manufacturing / Best Before

Month & Year of packaging and expiry declarations for perishable commodities.

Rule 6(1)(n)

Consumer Grievance Cell

Consumer care helpline phone number, email address, and postal contact details.

Rule 6(10) & USP

Country of Origin & USP

Country of origin declaration and mandatory Unit Sale Price for multi-pack/bulk goods.
